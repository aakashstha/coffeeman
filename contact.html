Common Questions

Good to Know.

Do you take reservations?

We're a walk-in café — no reservations needed. Just come on in! For large groups or catering enquiries, please send us a message via the form above.

Do you offer catering?

Yes! We offer catering for corporate events, meetings, and special occasions. Get in touch via the contact form or call us on 0408 230 058 to discuss your needs.

Do you have parking nearby?

There's street parking available on Mitchell Street and surrounding roads. The TCG Centre also has a car park. We're also an easy walk from Darwin City bus stops.

Are dairy-free and dietary options available?

Absolutely. We offer oat, almond, soy, and macadamia milk for all coffee drinks. Many of our menu items can be adjusted for dietary requirements — just ask our friendly team.

Do you serve food all day?

Yes — breakfast and our full menu is available from open until close. Whether you're in at 7am or 2:30pm, the kitchen is running and the coffee is fresh.
